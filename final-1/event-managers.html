05Planning the show

From brief to cue

Four stages to plan for. What to have ready at each one:

  1. 1

    Brief call

    The occasion, the audience, who speaks and in what order, and what the client wants the room to take away.

  2. 2

    Script

    Names and designations to announce, award categories, sponsor mentions, and any lines the client needs word for word.

  3. 3

    Rehearsal

    The tech rehearsal or venue walk-through time, and who calls the show.

  4. 4

    Run of show

    The latest running order, and who passes on changes on the day.

Planning an event?

A date, a city and the format are enough to start.
