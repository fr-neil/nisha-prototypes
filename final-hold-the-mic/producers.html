Bios, three lengths.

To paste into a programme, a script or an invitation.

Short about 25 words

Nisha Shetty is a corporate emcee based in Mumbai. She hosts in English and Hindi, across India and abroad, and has worked full‑time since 2016.

Medium about 60 words

Nisha Shetty is a corporate emcee based in Mumbai, hosting in English and Hindi. Full‑time since 2016, she hosts award nights, conferences, partner meets, launches, red carpets and pageants, on stage, on camera and online, across India and abroad. HSBC, Raychem RPG, TATA AIA Life and Himalaya are among the clients who have booked her again.

Long about 110 words

Nisha Shetty is a corporate emcee based in Mumbai. She hosts in English and Hindi, and has worked full‑time since 2016.

Her stages range from award nights, conferences and partner meets to launches, pageants and the red carpet at the Jio Filmfare Awards 2018. She also hosts on camera and online, from NSE India’s “Investment Demystified” series to virtual awards for ICICI Prudential Life.

Programmes since 2024 have taken her to Tokyo, Bali, Baku, Macao, Tbilisi, Colombo, Auckland and Queenstown, Ho Chi Minh City and Seoul. HSBC, Raychem RPG, TATA AIA Life and Himalaya are among the clients who have booked her again.
