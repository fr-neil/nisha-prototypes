Approved · Wordmark A 700, “Cue”

Her name in poster capitals (Bold 700, 79% width), closed with a small red cue card. The same card becomes the monogram, the main button and the one graphic device. Four parts and one red make the system.

Built from her own evidence: the capital-letter name on her 2026 “HOSTING IN” posters, the cue cards she holds on stage, and the saturated red she wears to host.

Two directions

Both are drawn from her own past devices. A is recommended.

A · Cue · approved (700 weight)

Condensed capitals and a red cue card

  • Hers: continues the capital-letter name of her 2026 posters (New Zealand, South Korea) and the cue cards seen across a decade of her stage photos.
  • Energy: bold, narrow capitals read in under a second and hold at 18 px in a header. The 700 weight was chosen over 800: less “broadcast”, same letter height.
  • One system: the red card is also the monogram, the button and the device. One ownable detail: the shared crossbar on “TT”.
  • Bilingual: the same family draws her name in Devanagari.
B · Mic-dot · alternative

A serif masthead with her mic dot

  • Hers: revives her own idea of a microphone standing in for the “i”, seen on her 2016 name card and her 2019 logo.
  • Against: the hairlines break up at small sizes, and a fashion-magazine serif pulls toward the lifestyle register that Phase 24 removed.
  • Against: it is quieter than the brief's “bold, confident, energetic”, and closer to P24's serif look.
  • Open: we do not know if she has retired her 2019 logo (question for Nisha).

Wordmark A

Hand-built from Anek Devanagari Bold 700 at 79% width (+0.02em tracking), converted to outlines. No font is needed to show it. The earlier ExtraBold 800 cut stays on file but is not used.

Clear space and minimum size

½ cap height

Keep half the cap height clear on every side. Smallest use: 96 px wide on screen, 25 mm in print. Below that, use the monogram.

Devanagari companion

For Hindi pages and print. Same family, same red card. Spelling “निशा शेट्टी” to be confirmed by Nisha

The device: Cue card

The card she hosts from, in her hand on stage for a decade. On the site it is a shape, not a scene: a small tilted card that carries a few words, most often her own poster line, “Hosting in”.

3 : 2, the shape of a 6×4 in. index card Tilt −3°. Never more than 4°
Hosting in Seoul Sep 2026
Kicker · place · one short line. Six words at most. Place names over 10 letters drop a size Radius 6 px. Shadow only over photos

The one main button is a red cue card. It rests level and tilts −2° only on hover, focus or press; the label stays level. The only secondary is a small text link to the same form: Just a question? Use the same form.

Sound sits on the media: an ink-on-white “▶ Hear her” control, never a coloured button beside the CTA. Cobalt appears only as the focus ring.
